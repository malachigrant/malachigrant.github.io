Classic Pound Cake

Rich, dense, and buttery — a timeless cake that rewards a little patience with an unforgettable crumb.

Serves12 slices
Prep25 min
Bake70–75 min
Oven350°F / 175°C

Ingredients

  • 2 cups (455 g) unsalted butter, softened
  • 3 ½ cups (700 g) granulated sugar
  • 6 large whole eggs1
  • 6 large egg yolks1 (in addition to the 6 whole eggs)
  • 1 Tablespoon vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 3 ½ cups (438 g) all-purpose (plain) flour2

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and generously grease (with shortening) and flour a 10-inch tube pan or 12-cup bundt pan (the pan must be able to hold 12 cups). Shake out excess flour and set aside.

  2. Place your butter in a large bowl and use an electric mixer to beat it until creamy and smooth.

    This recipe makes a lot of batter — a stand mixer is highly recommended.

  3. Scrape down the sides of the bowl and add the sugar. Beat until well combined and light and fluffy (about 1–2 minutes).

  4. In a separate, medium-sized bowl, combine your eggs, additional egg yolks, vanilla extract, and salt.

  5. Use a fork to lightly beat the eggs and yolks until all egg yolks are broken up and the salt and vanilla are incorporated.

  6. With the mixer on low speed, slowly pour the egg mixture into your batter, allowing it to gradually become incorporated.

    Don't pour it all in at once — if you can't manage a steady stream, add it in about 6 parts, letting each part become well incorporated before adding more. Once all is in, scrape down the sides and bottom, then increase to medium-high and beat for another 1–2 minutes.

  7. Reduce the mixer speed to low and gradually, about ¼ cup at a time, add the flour until all has been added.

  8. Scrape down the sides and bottom of the bowl and stir again on medium speed until well-combined, then beat for another minute.

  9. Spread the batter evenly into the prepared tube pan and use a spatula to smooth the surface.

  10. Transfer to the 350°F (175°C) oven and bake on the center rack for 1 hour 10 minutes to 1 hour 15 minutes, or until a wooden skewer inserted in the thickest part comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs.

    Do NOT over-bake this cake or it will be dry.

  11. Allow the cake to cool for at least 20 minutes, then run a knife around the sides of the tube pan and the center.

  12. Carefully invert the pound cake onto a cooling rack and allow it to cool completely before serving.

Notes

1 Eggs

You need a total of 12 eggs for this recipe, but you'll only use 6 whole eggs plus 6 additional egg yolks. That means you'll be discarding 6 egg whites — or save them to make meringue cookies or a white cake.

2 Cake Flour

You can substitute cake flour. If measuring by weight (which you really should be!), do a straight 1:1 substitute. If measuring by cups, you'll need 3 ¾ cup + 2 Tablespoons of cake flour. The cake turns out just as well — slightly softer and a smidge less dense. You do not need to add baking powder. DO NOT UNDER ANY CIRCUMSTANCES USE SELF-RISING FLOUR.
