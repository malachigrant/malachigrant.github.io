Crispy Waffles

Golden, crispy on the outside and tender on the inside

Serves
4
Prep Time
10 min
Rest Time
30 min
Difficulty
Easy

🥣 Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup cornstarch
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup whole milk or buttermilk
  • 1/3 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 egg
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons sugar
  • 3/4 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Butter and syrup, for serving

👨‍🍳 Directions

  1. In a medium bowl, combine the flour, cornstarch, baking powder, baking soda, and salt; mix well. Add the milk, vegetable oil, egg, sugar, and vanilla and mix well. Let the batter sit for 30 minutes.
  2. Preheat a waffle iron. Do not use non-stick spray on the waffle iron; the oil in the batter will allow the waffle to release easily.
  3. Follow the directions on your waffle iron to cook the waffles. Serve immediately with butter and syrup.
💡 Pro Tip: The cornstarch is the secret to extra-crispy waffles. Letting the batter rest for 30 minutes allows the starches to hydrate fully, giving you that perfect golden crunch.
⚠️ Important: Never use non-stick spray on the waffle iron. The oil already in the batter is all you need for easy release.
🧈 Serve immediately with butter and syrup 🍯
