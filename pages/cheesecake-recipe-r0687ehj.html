Cheesecake

A classic New York-style cheesecake with a graham cracker crust

Crust

  • 1.5 cups graham cracker crumbs
  • 4 tbsp (50g) melted butter
  • 2 tbsp sugar
Bake: 350°F for 8–10 minutes

Cake

  • 4 blocks cream cheese (room temperature)
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 0.5 tsp almond extract
  • 1/3 cup heavy cream
  • 4 eggs
  1. Beat cream cheese and sugar together.
  2. Add cream and extracts. Mix until fully smooth.
  3. Add eggs one at a time — mix just until incorporated (don't overbeat).
  4. Pour into pan onto the crust. Gently tap the pan on the counter a few times to release trapped air.
  5. Bake at 300°F for 60 minutes, then immediately turn off the oven and crack the door open for an additional 30 minutes.
  6. Turn off the oven. Leave the cheesecake inside with the door cracked open for 1 hour.
  7. Remove from oven and let cool completely.
  8. Place in the fridge to set.

Enjoy! Let it chill overnight for the best texture.
