Classic Cheesecake

Serves 12 | Prep Time: 20 min | Bake Time: 60 min

Ingredients

For the Crust

  • 2 cups graham cracker crumbs
  • 1/4 cup melted butter
  • 3 tablespoons sugar

For the Filling

  • 32 oz (4 blocks) cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 325°F (160°C). Prepare a 9-inch springform pan.
  2. In a medium bowl, combine graham cracker crumbs, melted butter, and sugar. Press mixture firmly into the bottom and up the sides of the pan. Bake for 10 minutes. Cool completely.
  3. In a large bowl, beat cream cheese and sugar until smooth and creamy.
  4. Beat in sour cream and vanilla extract.
  5. On medium speed, beat in eggs one at a time, scraping down the sides of the bowl.
  6. Stir in flour until just combined.
  7. Pour filling over the prepared crust.
  8. Bake for 55-60 minutes, or until the center is just set (it should still jiggle slightly).
  9. Turn off the oven and crack the door open. Let the cheesecake cool in the oven for 1 hour.
  10. Remove from oven and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight before serving.
Tip: To prevent cracks, make sure your cream cheese is at room temperature and don't overbeat the eggs. The water bath method also helps prevent cracking.
