Fluffy Buttermilk Pancakes

Light, golden, and impossibly fluffy — the classic breakfast stack, ready in under 20 minutes.

⏱ 20 min 🍽 Serves 4 📊 Easy 🔥 420 kcal / serving
🥞🥞🥞

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 cups buttermilk
  • 2 large eggs
  • 3 tbsp melted butter
  • Vanilla extract (optional)
  • Maple syrup, for serving

Instructions

  1. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt together in a large bowl.
  2. In a separate bowl, beat the eggs, then stir in the buttermilk, melted butter, and vanilla.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and stir until just combined. A few lumps are fine — don't overmix.
  4. Let the batter rest for 5 minutes so it thickens and the bubbles form.
  5. Heat a griddle or nonstick pan over medium and lightly grease with butter.
  6. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter per pancake. Cook until bubbles appear and edges look set, about 2–3 minutes.
  7. Flip and cook another 1–2 minutes until golden brown.
  8. Stack, drizzle with maple syrup, and serve warm.

Pro Tips

  • Don't overmix — lumps keep the pancakes fluffy.
  • Resting the batter is the secret to tall, airy stacks.
  • Keep finished pancakes warm in a 200°F (95°C) oven.
  • No buttermilk? Mix 2 cups milk with 2 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar and let sit 5 minutes.
