Lime Cheesecake

Bright, tangy, and perfectly creamy with a graham cracker crust

Crust

  • 1.5 cups graham cracker crumbs
  • 4 tbsp (50g) melted butter
  • 2 tbsp sugar
Bake: 350°F for 8–10 minutes

Cake

  • 3 blocks cream cheese (room temperature)
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 tbsp corn starch
  • 2/3 cup heavy cream
  • 4 eggs
  • 1/2 cup lime juice
  • 1 tbsp lime zest
  • 1.5 tsp vanilla extract
  1. Beat cream cheese, sugar, and corn starch together until smooth.
  2. Add heavy cream, lime juice, lime zest, and vanilla. Mix until fully smooth.
  3. Add eggs one at a time — mix just until incorporated (don't overbeat).
  4. Pour into pan onto the crust. Gently tap the pan on the counter a few times to release trapped air.
  5. Bake at 300°F for 60 minutes, then turn off the oven and crack the door open for an additional 30 minutes.
  6. Remove from oven and let cool completely.
  7. Place in the fridge to set.

Enjoy! Let it chill overnight for the best texture.
