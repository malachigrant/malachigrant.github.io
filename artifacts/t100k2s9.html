Homemade Pizza

Crackly crust, stretchy cheese, and fresh toppings — the classic Margherita, done right.

⏱ 1 hr 15 min 👥 Serves 2–4 🔥 250°C / 480°F 📊 Medium

Ingredients

  • 500 g bread flour (00 flour is ideal)
  • 325 ml warm water
  • 7 g fresh yeast (or 3 g dry)
  • 10 g fine sea salt
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 200 g crushed San Marzano tomatoes
  • 250 g fresh mozzarella, torn
  • 10 leaves fresh basil
  • 1 pinch sugar
  • — semolina, for dusting

Method

  1. Make the dough. Dissolve the yeast in warm water, add flour and salt, then knead for 10 minutes until smooth and elastic. Add olive oil at the end.
  2. First rise. Cover the bowl with a damp cloth and let the dough double in size, about 1 hour, in a warm spot.
  3. Shape the pizzas. Punch down the dough, divide into 2 balls, and let them rest 15 minutes. On a semolina-dusted board, stretch each ball from the center outward, leaving a puffy rim.
  4. Make the sauce. Mix crushed tomatoes with a pinch of salt, sugar, and a drizzle of olive oil. Keep it simple — no cooking needed.
  5. Top. Spread a thin layer of sauce, scatter torn mozzarella, and add a few basil leaves. Finish with a drizzle of olive oil.
  6. Bake hot. Preheat the oven to its maximum (250°C / 480°F) with a pizza stone or inverted baking tray inside, for at least 30 minutes. Bake the pizza for 8–10 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbling.
  7. Finish. Remove from the oven, add the remaining basil, and serve immediately.

Pro Tips

  • A preheated pizza stone (or inverted tray) is the closest thing to a wood-fired oven you can buy.
  • Don't overload the toppings — a thin, even layer is the secret to a crisp base.
  • Pat the mozzarella dry with paper towels so the dough doesn't get soggy.
  • For a deeper flavor, refrigerate the dough up to 48 hours before baking.
The best pizza is eaten within two minutes of leaving the oven. Set the table before you start baking!
